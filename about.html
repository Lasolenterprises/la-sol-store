Our Story

Rooted on Oʻahu, Grown in Kona

Lā Sol Enterprises was founded on Oʻahu with a simple goal: share authentic 100% Kona coffee with anyone who loves a great cup, whether they live down the street or on the other side of the world.

"Lā" is the Hawaiian word for sun — a nod to the sun-drenched slopes of the Kona districts on Hawaiʻi Island, where volcanic soil, cloud cover, and gentle rain create the exact conditions Kona coffee is famous for. We work to bring that same island quality to every bag we roast and ship.

Based in Oʻahu, we're proud to serve our local community with fresh, direct orders — and just as proud to ship that same coffee across the U.S. and around the world, so the taste of Hawaiʻi can reach any coffee lover, anywhere.

Every order is a small piece of the islands, delivered straight to your door.
